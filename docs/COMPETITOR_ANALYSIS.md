### 1. Executive Comparison Overview
Krishna's Herbal & Ayurveda represents a leading D2C Ayurvedic wellness brand in India, built on Shopify with a sophisticated, conversion-optimized storefront. Our audit of Krishna Ayurveda reveals exact patterns that drive multi-crore GMV in the Indian herbal wellness space.

### 3. Key UX Patterns to Adopt from Krishna Ayurveda

#### A. "Shop by Health Concern" as Primary Gateway
Ayurvedic customers do not search for "Syrup" or "Tablet"; they search for solutions to physiological discomforts. Krishna places "Shop by Concern" as the prominent first section on their homepage and main navigation:
- *Digestive Wellness*, *Diabetic Care*, *Joint & Pain Relief*, *Liver Care*, *Immunity*, *Women's Wellness*, *Men's Health*.
- **Fibax Implementation**: Group Fibax's 35 products into 10 intuitive concern hubs with dedicated landing pages optimized for search intent and paid ad landing traffic.

#### B. High-Converting Product Card Anatomy
Krishna's product cards combine:
1. Primary product packshot on a clean neutral background.
2. Discount badge (e.g., "-25% OFF") and social proof badge (e.g., "Bestseller").
3. Product title with pack size/volume clearly indicated.
4. Star rating with exact review count (e.g., ★ 4.8 (142 reviews)).
5. Dual pricing: Strike-through MRP + Bold Green Sale Price.
6. Direct "Add to Cart" button without forcing the user to open the PDP.

#### C. Frictionless Cart Drawer (Slide-Over)
Instead of redirecting shoppers to a slow full-page cart, Krishna utilizes an instant side drawer:
- Visual progress bar: *"Add ₹240 more to unlock FREE Shipping!"*
- Real-time cart total recalculation.
- Mini product recommendations based on cart contents (e.g., adding Amla Powder if buying Immunity Syrup).
- Direct "Proceed to Checkout" button that bypasses unnecessary steps.

#### D. Product Page (PDP) Information Architecture
Krishna organizes complex herbal knowledge into digestible chunks:
- Hero section: Gallery + Purchase options + Pack size variants.
- "Why You'll Love It" bullet points.
- "Top Active Ingredients": Visual circles/cards featuring herbs (Amla, Giloy, Ashwagandha, Shilajit) with their ancient Ayurvedic therapeutic rationale.
- "How to Use" with dosage, timing (Before/After Meals), and course duration.
- "Frequently Asked Questions" collapsible accordion.
- Customer Reviews with photo/video proof and ratings filter.

### 4. How Fibax Will Differentiate & Surpass Krishna
While Krishna Ayurveda offers a solid benchmark, Fibax must not clone it. Fibax will differentiate strategically:

1. **Modern Botanical Minimalist Aesthetic**:
   - Krishna's site can feel cluttered with excessive marketing popups and aggressive badges. Fibax will use clean whitespace, refined typography, elegant herbal illustrations, and a calmer, premium health aesthetic.
2. **Superior Performance & Speed**:
   - Built on React 18, Vite, and modern edge CDN delivery, the new Fibax site will load in under 1 second, providing a noticeable speed advantage over heavy Shopify app setups.
3. **Synergistic Formulations (Syrup + Capsule Dual Therapies)**:
   - Fibax possesses unique combination therapies (e.g., *Livupchar Syrup + Livupchar Capsules*, *Axe Ortho Oil + Axe Ortho Capsules + Axe Ortho Syrup*). Positioning these as scientifically paired "Complete Healing Courses" will create a distinct market edge.
4. **Enhanced Transparency**:
   - Clear display of standardization percentages, batch testing guarantees, and transparent herbal extracts without artificial colors or hidden heavy metals.
